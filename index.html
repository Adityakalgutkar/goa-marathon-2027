Event
overview

Every year, runners, families and schools take over the streets of Panjim. Some come to chase a personal best over 42.2 km, others to finish their first 5 km in costume.

After 13 editions and more than 10,000 runners, Goa Marathon is still about one thing: running together for children in Goa who need safe homes, education and hope.

Every kilometre is for them.

Goa Marathon is organised in support of El Shaddai Charitable Trust, which gives children in Goa safe homes, education and a future to run towards. Your entry is part of that work.
