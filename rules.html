Entry rules.

Entry rules and regulations.

Race day: Sunday, 24 January 2027. Applications close on 15 January 2027. Please read the entry conditions and disclaimer before applying.

Entry Rules and Regulations – Individual Category

  1. Only one applicant per form can apply for the event. Also do not send in more than one entry per person; multiple application will entail all being rejected.
  2. Application can be done online at www.goamarathon.org.
  3. Application can also be done through pre-printed application forms available at the Marathon Office.
  4. Payment can be done via demand draft/cheque in the name of El Shaddai Charitable Trust; and sent to the Marathon Office.
  5. The last date for receipt of application forms at the Marathon Office/Online is 15th January 2027
  6. Applications must not be submitted to any person or group other than at the Marathon Office. Incomplete entries will be rejected.
  7. An applicant must affix his/her latest coloured photograph on the application form (or attach a jpeg if registering online). The applicants' face must be clearly visible in the photograph.
  8. An entry form must be accompanied with requisite fee as per the race category i.e. Rs. 2350/- for Full Marathon, Rs. 1850/-for Half Marathon, Rs. 1350 for 10K Run, Rs. 600/- for the 5Km Dream Run and Rs. 200/- for the School Run. A form received without the correct fee or no fee will be rejected.
  9. For entry acceptance, the Disclaimer; must be signed. If the applicant is below the age of 18 years, his/her guardian must also sign the disclaimer and write his/her relation with the application on the form.
  10. Entries not accepted: Participation in the event is subject to entry confirmation. If an entry is not confirmed for any reason an amount of Rs. 500 will be deducted as processing charges.
  11. Entry Cancellation/Refunds: Any fee discrepancy must be brought to the attention of the Event Registration Team immediately. Refund queries will not be entertained post 05th January 2027.
  12. Bib Expo for collection of running Bibs and timing chips will be held at SAG Ground, Campal, Panaji, on 21, 22 and 23 Jan 2027, from 10 A.M. to 6 P.M.
  13. For Bib collection carry acknowledgment slip/Printout of the email confirmation sent by us / printout of website confirmation.
  14. Authorized representatives to carry:
    • Original acceptance letter
    • Signed authorization letter from confirmed participant
    • Photocopy of photo-identity proof (of the confirmed participant), e.g. Aadhaar card, Passport, PAN Card, Election Card or Driving License.
  15. Participants are encouraged to raise pledges either offline or online by creating an online fund raising page on www.goamarathon.org.
  16. T-shirts for the 42K, 21K and 10K are included in the pack.
  17. For the 5K, runners can buy a T-shirt at the venue for Rs. 500/- or can design their own, for which there is an award.

Application Instructions – School Run

  1. Only one applicant per form can apply for the event. Do not send in more than one entry per person. Multiple application will entail all being rejected.
  2. Application can be done only offline at the Akbarally Building, 2nd Floor, Opp. Junta House, 18th June Road, Panjim, Goa – 403001 India.
  3. Payment can be done via Cash/ UPI in the name of "El Shaddai Charitable Trust" and sent to the Event Office.
  4. The last date for receipt of application forms at the Event Office is 15th January 2027.
  5. Applications must not be submitted to any person or group other than the collection centers or Akbarally Building, 2nd Floor, Opp. Junta House, 18th June Road, Panjim, Goa – 403001 India. Incomplete entries will be rejected.
  6. An applicant must affix his/her latest coloured photograph on the application form. The applicant's face must be clearly visible in the photograph.
  7. An entry form for School Run must be accompanied with requisite fee of Rs. 200/-. A form received without the correct fee or no fee will be rejected.
  8. For entry acceptance, the 'Disclaimer' must be signed by his/her guardian and write his/her relation with the applicant.
  9. Entries not accepted: Participation in the event is subject to entry confirmation. If an entry is not confirmed for any reason an amount of Rs. 100 will be deducted as processing charges.
  10. Entry Cancellation/Refunds: Any fee discrepancy must be brought to the attention of the Event Registration Team immediately. Refund queries will not be entertained post 05th January 2027.
  11. Bib Expo for collection will be held at SAG Ground – Campal on the January 21, 22 & 23 2027, from 10am to 6pm
  12. For Bib collection carry acknowledgment slip
  13. Authorized representatives to carry:
    • Original acknowledgment slip
    • Signed authorization letter from confirmed participant
    • Photocopy of photo-identity proof (of the confirmed participant), e.g. Aadhaar card or School ID card
