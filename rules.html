Entry rules.

What every runner needs to know before signing up and on race morning.

Before you enter.

Please read these rules before you register. By entering, you agree to follow them on race day.

  1. Entries close on 15 January 2027. Registration is online through runINDIA.
  2. Every race starts and finishes at the Sports Authority of Goa (SAG) Ground, Campal, Panjim.
  3. Collect your bib at the Bib Expo, SAG Ground, Campal (21 to 23 January 2027, 10am to 6pm), and wear it on the front of your T-shirt on race day.
  4. Start and reporting times for each distance will be announced on the Races page.
