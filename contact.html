When and where is the race?

Sunday, 24 January 2027. Every race starts and finishes at the Sports Authority of Goa (SAG) Ground, Campal, Panjim.

When do entries close?

15 January 2027. Register online through runINDIA.

What time does my race start?

Start and reporting times for each distance will be announced on this page soon.

Where do I collect my bib and T-shirt?

At the Bib Expo, SAG Ground, Campal, from 21 to 23 January 2027, 10am to 6pm.

Is a T-shirt included?

Yes for the 42.2 km, 21.1 km and 10 km. For the 5K Dream Run, you can buy one at the venue for ₹500, or design your own.

How do I find my results?

Results will be published after race day, searchable by bib number.
