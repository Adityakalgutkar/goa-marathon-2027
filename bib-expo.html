Bib Expo

Bib Expo 2027.

Collect your bib and T-shirt at SAG Ground, Campal, from Thursday 21 to Saturday 23 January 2027, 10am to 6pm.

Collect your race kit.

Every registered runner collects their bib and T-shirt at the Bib Expo before race day. Your bib is your entry to the start line, so don’t miss it.

Venue

SAG Ground, Campal, Panjim

Dates

Thursday 21, Friday 22 and Saturday 23 January 2027

Opening hours

10am to 6pm, all three days

What’s in your kit.

What you collect depends on your race.

42.2 km, 21.1 km and 10 km

5K Dream Run

Your race bib. The T-shirt is optional at ₹500 if you chose it when you registered.
